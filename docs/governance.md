# Governance and Decision Rights

| Decision | Accountable owner | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow and service-level definitions | Operations lead | Weekly operating review |
| Availability, permissions and audit logging | Technology owner | Least privilege and launch verification |
| Policy content and external communications | Client authority | Human approval before publication |
| P1/P2 closure | Site supervisor | Explicit supervisor confirmation |
| Taxonomy or automation changes | Change-control group | Impact review, regression test, documented sign-off |

AI can summarize, translate, or suggest a category. It cannot close emergencies, invent policy, make contractual decisions, or replace the accountable human.
