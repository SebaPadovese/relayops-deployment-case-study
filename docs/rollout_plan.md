# Six-Week Rollout Plan

| Week | Objective | Outputs | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery and baseline | Interviews, current-state map, sample incidents, risks | Problem and owners agreed |
| 2 | Configuration | Taxonomy, priorities, roles, required fields, privacy rules | Design sign-off |
| 3 | Prototype and data setup | Forms, queues, notifications, knowledge content | Internal walkthrough passed |
| 4 | UAT and training | Test scripts, defect log, supervisor training, quick guide | No open critical defects |
| 5 | Two-site pilot | Daily stand-up, adoption tracking, routing review | Pilot metrics stable |
| 6 | Phased go-live | Wave rollout, hypercare, handover, operating review | Business owner accepts service |

## Launch criteria

- All P1/P2 paths pass acceptance testing.
- Permissions, auditability and offline fallback are verified.
- Supervisors can reassign and close cases.
- Critical defects are zero.
- Support, escalation and ownership are accepted by the business owner.

## Proposed post-launch measures

Time to acknowledgement, backlog age, escalation accuracy, first-time completeness and trained-user adoption. These are proposed metrics, not achieved customer outcomes.
